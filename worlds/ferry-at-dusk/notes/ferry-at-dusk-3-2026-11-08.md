# ferry-at-dusk-3, playtest notes, 2026-11-08

Nate plays Hob the miller. The Traveler is a mind (principal `traveler`, a CLI subagent of the maintainer session). Narrator: a side session, main principal `claude` joined as Hob.

## Setup

- `worlds()`: `ferry-at-dusk-3` exists, `forked_from` world `ferry-at-dusk-2` at seq 375. Its `principals` count read 1 before I joined (the Traveler mind via the CLI), but `principals()` on this MCP connection listed only `claude`. Expected, since the CLI is another process, but it means the narrator cannot see from its own connection who else is in the world except through `embodied_by` in perceive.
- Join at t=189.4: Hob stands in Ashford Square, not at the mill. Present: Mara, the Traveler (embodied by `traveler`), Biscuit, the notice board, a quarter mug of dark ale, the well, the bench.
- Gap: the brief for this game says Hob has rope, flour and bread, but `holding` is empty. Hob's card has no goals either (the Traveler has four). An NPC seat with no goals gives the player nothing to pursue.
- Policy: `fill: true`, `voice: true`. `fixed` still carries the first night's timings (fog at t=90, night at t=150, dawn at t=400); the real timers are fog at 422, nightfall 472, dawn 722. The fixed text is stale after two forks.
- Odd affordance: `sleep` is listed with targets Mara and the Traveler (ok: true). Looks like a holder verb leaking an object target. Not tried.
- As author, `recall(about="Traveler")` from Hob shows the Traveler's secret memory and goals. Right for an author, but when the main principal is also the player's seat the narrator must take care not to leak it into the fiction.

## traveler2's own report (left after about 20 of 40 waits, t near 325)

Closed g3 (Tam's jugs, by the day's work) and g4 (Biscuit, kept by the Traveler, who will feed him), with `remember` at each step. It left early because its context was running out. Its engine findings, which I have not all checked:

1. Missed line: Hob's "They're just in the cupboard behind you." (seq 576, addressed to it) never came back from a `wait`; it found it later only in `knows.last_exchanges`. It thinks its own `act` moves its cursor past other people's events in between. Same family as my own narrator miss above: act results should carry what touched you since your cursor.
2. Events leak across places: while in Pell's Mill, `perceive(cursor=612)` showed Mara's say to Hob and her give in the Square (seq 619 to 625).
3. Raw `patched` events for Hob's `drinks` (1 to 4) reached the Traveler's feed. Author bookkeeping visible to a player.
4. Times out of order (the two clocks bug above) and `go` results say `turn: 5` while `clock.turn` is 3 (`go` duration 5 probably; the field name misleads).
5. The Traveler spoke its default "Hm." while nobody embodied it (after the first mind left). Correct by design, but the mind reads it later as its own words.
6. `wait` is too heavy: a timed out wait with no events still returns the whole bundle (about 12k tokens: affordances, memory, exchanges). A 40 wait loop eats a mind's context halfway. Wait should return only new events plus a short header, with a flag for the full bundle.
7. No chore verbs (same as the mill gap above); `drop` cannot say where a thing goes.
8. The Bench still says "Something under it." though the bone was taken on the first night. Stale description.
 The Traveler's goal "pay Mara her story" can only be a `say` of the story itself, and the mind used a `talk` topic instead, which a table cannot recognise as payment.
