### Nate's direction to the GM (turn 12): what a character should be aware of

Characters should be aware of what they would naturally know and not of what they should not. Mara should know what time it is whenever she takes a turn, or be able to check it. Nate did not fix the shape. My proposal for the engine:
- Ambient facts: a small set of world-level state (time of day, weather, season) that every `perceive` includes for everyone, derived from the clock and the root. Dawn, dusk and fog become changes to that state, not events at one place, so a mind in the square knows the fog lifted at the landing because "the fog is a river thing everyone sees".
- Senses by reach: events already have `reach`. Add per-kind defaults (a bell is heard everywhere, a whisper only by its target, daylight everywhere) so the author does not have to remember them per call.
- Knowledge, not perception: some things a character knows without seeing (Mara knows Tam drinks at the mill). That is `brief` and `memory`. Keep it separate from what perceive shows.
- Secrets stay out: `remembered`, `brief`, `dialog`, `gives` never appear in another entity's feed (see the leak above).
- A `time()` or a `clock` field in every perceive (`t`, day count, phase like dawn/day/dusk/night) so a mind can check the time at any moment.
