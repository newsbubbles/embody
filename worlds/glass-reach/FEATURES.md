# What's new in the Glass Reach

New things in the world, newest first, for players and visitors. This is not the engine's changelog: each entry is
something you can find, use or run into in the Reach. Everything here is also met in the world itself, usually
through someone who explains it. No spoilers: a mystery stays out of this list until players have found it.

Format: one `## date: title` heading per entry (date is the real-world day it went live), then two or three
sentences. `status: coming` under a heading means it is built or being built and not live yet.

## 2026-11-14: Belt wards from the House

The dawn crawler after the moon brought a long black oak case to the Hold under House Varrick's seal: six belt wards
from the House's own store, with a letter from the steward to the Reeve. A ward that is switched on turns aside every
quick blow and every shot; only a slow, heavy blow gets through it. The Reeve gives them to the men who stand nearest
him.

## 2026-11-14: One health bar, and death that stays

Health is one number now, and how you are doing reads in plain words. Down is not dead, and
someone can still bring you back up. Dead is dead: the body stays where it fell and nobody plays it again. Thirst no
longer just weakens you. Go without water long enough and it kills you, slowly, in one to three days depending on how
hardy your body is.

## 2026-11-13: Beasts on the land

The thornbacks of the brake are animals now, not scenery. They grow from pup to boar and sow, eat the roots under the
glassthorn, breed at the moon and starve when the brake runs short. Grub a root by hand and you have something to offer
one: with patience and a feel for survival, a thornback can be tamed and kept, and a tamed one guards. Idris at the
steading remembers old Tessel raising pups.

## 2026-11-13: The Keth goats

The Keth keep a small herd of rust-red goats on Red Ridge, each with a knot of blue cord in one ear. They graze the
ridge, bear kids, and the nannies give milk you can drink. Ask Taan or Iska about them before you try to take one.

## 2026-11-13: The seep fills a mended cistern

Mend the cistern at the Gate and the seep in the rock fills it again over time. Water you can hold is worth a wall.

## 2026-11-13: Followers bring their own

When you walk and someone follows you, whoever follows them comes too, all the way down the line. A party moves as one.

## 2026-11-12: Locked stores and lights

Each side keeps a locked store at its home, open to those who know the way in and to anyone who can break it. Lamps
and fires can be lit, and dark places look different with a light in hand.

## 2026-11-12: The Keeper's book

The Hold's Keeper keeps a book that can set two written papers side by side and tell whether the same hand wrote them.
A charge laid in it goes before the Hold. Some people who saw things will now tell what they saw, to those who ask
the right way.

## 2026-11-12: A lime kiln for the wall

Lime holds the wall back: the Guild's sacks cost a shard each, and there are only three. Jory Flint, the mason, has
chalked a kiln beside the wall line that the yard can build as a works. Lit with Scar stone and glassthorn cut in the
brake, it burns six hours into a sack of lime. Cut the thorn by day.

## 2026-11-12: Tally slates

Ledgers you carry. The Guild's dark-glass tally slates, the houses' scratch-iron slates and the Keth count-cords keep
their holder's accounts: water, men, stone, shards. A slate notes what you give, take and hire on its own; you can
page, search and sort it, and total it with averages and spreads. What is on it is for its holder's eyes only; the
room sees only that you checked it. A Guild slate counts a room at a glance and sends word through the Counting House,
where the Guild sees who wrote to whom but never what. A house slate counts slowly and goes face to face. A Keth cord
cannot count a room at all.

## 2026-11-12: Hauler Nine drives the road

The Guild's crawler is a real vehicle now. Climb aboard in the Guild Yard, and whoever holds the wheel drives it
down the Salt Road, past the stops, with everyone aboard riding along. Hear it coming before you see it.

## 2026-11-12: The truce answers to who broke it
A dig on the Flats by the Hold's own people still strains the truce with the Keth. A dig by anyone else is a grudge
the Keth hold against that side, not the Hold. A broken truce can be mended.

## 2026-11-12: House Dreyer's skins
House Dreyer keeps filled water skins at the Dry Camp. A skin of water is a wage: it signs a drifter to the house.

## 2026-11-12: The Deepwell cups
Stone cups on cords hang at the Keth's black pool. Anyone may drink from them, and they never run dry.

## 2026-11-12: The moon is coming
The Reach counts down to the moon's turn: dawn signs, posted notices with the live count of each side, talk and
rumours in the camps. Some of the rumours are true.

## 2026-11-12: Writing
Blank leaves can be written on and handed over. Letters weigh nothing in the hands. Paper burns at the hearth and
stays burned.

## 2026-11-11: The Guild Yard and its locks
The Carriers' Guild has a yard on the road, with its four keepers, a strongbox and locked doors. Locks can be picked
or broken, given the skill and the time.

## 2026-11-11: New ground
The Riders' Hollow, the Thornbrake, Tessel's Steading and Weeping Rock open to explore. West of the Scar is still a
mystery.

## 2026-11-11: The wall goes up
The Hold builds its wall: dig glass, dress the stone, lay the courses.

## 2026-11-11: Drifters sign on
One drifter a day will sign with each house, for pay.

## 2026-11-11: Chapter two, the standoff
House Dreyer camps at the Dry Camp. The Salt Works, the convoy, the cistern and the herald come into play, and the

## 2026-11-11: Hidden finds and kits
