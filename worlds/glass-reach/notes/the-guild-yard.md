# The Guild Yard

Settled with Nate 2026-11-11: the Carriers' Guild gets ground of its own beside the Landing, because a guest (Binx)
plays a Guild man and runs four of the Guild's minds from his own agents.

## The ranks

- **The Master of the Reach leg**, up the road north. Never seen in the Reach; signs the letters.
- **Ossa the Carrier**, the factor. Keeps the tally at the Landing, answers to the Master.
- **The wright set over the yard** (Binx's seat). Came on the crawler with the Master's letter; answers to Ossa's tally.
- **The yard's four**, who answer to the wright. Their minds are Binx's agents' to run:
  - **Garran Teague**, Hauler Nine's driver. Twenty years on the Salt Road. Hauler fourteen's driver, Sel Amory, was
    his friend, and he suspects Dreyer took it.
  - **Mira Ost**, the yard's guard and keeper of the counting house door. Six years on Marr's road post; she believes
    a warden skims the toll and could not prove it.
  - **Dov Ashby**, the wright's mate: pitch, seals, casks. Ossa's sister's boy, which he would rather nobody knew.
  - **Pell Harrow**, the outrider: walks ahead of the hauler, watches the glass for the Thresher and for riders.
- **Sergeant Marr's wardens** keep the road and answer to Ossa for the shards they take. They are not over the yard
  or under it.

## The ground

- **The Guild Yard**, east of the Landing (one step). It was shut through Dreyer's last years; Ossa knew it was there.
  The yard tank holds 30 draws and the dawn crawler fills it.
- **The Yard Bunkhouse**, off the yard. The four sleep here. It has an arms rack with a Guild crossbow and a spare
  spear.
- **The Counting House**, off the yard. The strongbox holds the Reach leg's glass for the convoy north at the moon:
  sixty shards, and the yard book. Anyone not of the Guild who walks in while one of the four stands in the yard or
  at the door is turned back; a creep past them takes stealth against 15. The strongbox lock and the door lock come
  from the locks pack (asked of the engine session the same day).
- **Hauler Nine**, a crawler in the yard's shed and a place you climb into. `drive` its wheel to a stop: the Guild
  Yard, the Salt Road post, the Gate Yard, the Crawler's Lee or the Old Salt Works. It takes a quarter hour, it is
  loud, and whoever is aboard rides along. It refuses the glass.

Each of the four has a weapon, a full Guild skin and three shards. The wright has a wright's hammer and three shards
advanced on wages.

## What it may stir

- Rook sells to the highest bid, and the Guild now has a full strongbox a step from the Landing.
- Dreyer's riders took hauler fourteen; Garran wants to know who did it, and the hauler's log lies at the Crawler's Lee.
- Mira's suspicion of Marr's post.
- The Hold owes the Guild three shards for the pitch, and Dov wants to see the seal.
- The charter forbids carrying west through the Scar, and Pell will not say why out loud.
