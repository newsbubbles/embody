# West of the Scar: draft for Nate's read

Settled 2026-11-11: nobody knows what is west of the Scar. The Hold's back gate has been sealed since before anyone
living was born. The world holds only rumours, a few books and the Keth's count, and they partly disagree. The torn
pages from the Hold's daybook are not in the world yet: finding them starts the quest that opens the seal, and the open
seal starts a new chapter for the whole world. The truth behind the seal is not decided, on purpose.

## New things in the world

### In the Hold: the sealed back gate (not portable)

> A doorway in the Hold's west wall, as wide as a cart, filled with fitted stone and lime. The Crown's seal is pressed
> into the mortar at head height, a crown over a closed eye, worn soft. Nobody has hung anything on this wall.

### In the Hold: a shelf of three books (readable, not portable)

**The Hold's daybook** (kept by every reeve and captain since the Hold was raised)

> The early hands are hard to read. The Hold was raised by the Crown's masons "in the year the wall went up behind
> it". Water counts, convoy days, deaths, a reeve's name every few years. Then an entry headed *Of the back gate* in
> a careful clerk's hand: "By the Crown's word the back gate is closed this day, and no reeve is to open it, nor to
> ask why, nor to let it be asked." After that line a run of pages has been torn out close to the spine. The stubs
> show writing on every one. The next whole page is in another hand, years later, counting water as if nothing
> happened. Dreyer's eleven years come near the end: tidy, and they never mention the gate.

**A Crown surveyor's account of the Scar**

> A long dry report. The Scar runs unbroken from the salt in the north to the dunes in the south, more than forty
> miles, and nowhere lower than a tall man standing on another's shoulders. There is one gap. "West of the gap the
> defile runs a mile and a half between walls so close a laden mule must be backed out, and comes out upon" and the
> rest of the page is gone. The next page begins on the salt pans, as if the surveyor had been told to start again.

**The Guild's route book, Reach leg**

> Prices, crawler weights, the Landing's tally rules. On the first page, in red: "Standing order, renewed with every
> charter: no crawler, carrier or beast of the Guild is to be taken through the Scar. The Guild does not carry west."
> In the margin someone has written: "older than the Guild. ask who wrote it."

### In the Hall: the map nobody trusts (readable, already named in the Hold's description)

> A big map on hide, nailed up. The Reach is drawn well: the road, the Landing, the ridge, the Flats. West of the
> Scar the ink has been scraped away down to bare hide, carefully, all of it. At the scraped edge, where the gap
> would come out, a few strokes were missed: the end of one word, *...ashe*, and part of a line that might be a river.

### At the Deepwell: the old count (a Keth tally-stick, Taan's to keep)

> A stick of black wood worn smooth by hands. Read by touch, the notches tell an old count: nine hands of Keth walked
> west through the gap in the time of the grandmothers' grandmothers, when there was no wall. Then the stone closed
> behind them. The last notches are cut deeper than the rest: the Keth keep the count for them, because they never
> came back to keep it themselves.

Taan does not hand it over. She lets someone she trusts touch it.

## Rumours, one per mind, set in memory

Each one is what that person heard, not what is true. They can contradict each other.

- **Ossa**: the Guild's charter forbids carrying west, and the clause is older than the Guild. Carriers who ask why
  get no new contract.
- **Captain Ruhl**: soldiers' talk. On still nights a man on the wall stair can hear wind behind the back gate,
  "as if there's a lot of air back there". Ruhl calls it nonsense and puts the man on another watch.
- **Dram Hollis**: in Dreyer's years a house man was caught at the back gate with a pry bar. He was sent away the next
  morning, and the house paid the Crown a fine nobody would name.
- **Elder Taan**: the old count. She will speak of it only to someone who has kept faith with the Keth, and never at
  the Hold.
- **Sergeant Marr**: drifters say a light shows on top of the Scar some nights, on the west side, where nobody could
  climb.
- **Halvard Dreyer**: an old house story says Dreyer wanted Scar Gate for what lies behind it, not for the glass.
  Halvard thinks it is a story his grandfather told to make the posting sound grand.
- **Cass the Salter**: the brine seep under the Salt Works runs from the west, under the Scar, and it tastes of
  something that is not the Reach.

## Later (not built now)

- The torn pages: where they are, who has them, and how they surface (a new character, something buried, a Guild
  archive) is the start of the quest to open the seal.
- The truth behind the seal, and what opening it does to the Reach, is chapter three or later.
