# Who nailed the notice by the cistern: the Host

Settled with Nate 2026-11-11: the notice is explained in the world, and the explanation can be found. It came from a
finding (FINDINGS.md, "The host's note became a mystery"): a patch note nailed into the fiction on the ninth was read as
fiction, and the reeve's session asked who wrote it.

## The answer

The Host: the Crown's sworn water-wardens. They ride the Reach by night under the Crown's closed-eye seal, the same seal
pressed into the mortar of the sealed back gate, and keep the water law without showing themselves. On the night of the
ninth they raised the three the thirst had put down (Captain Ruhl in the yard, Nico and Sefa on the ridge), filled their
skins, and nailed the ration notice at the cistern in their own cant. The notice's odd words ("agents at the gate",
"your human") are that cant. What the Host answers to, and whether it has to do with what lies behind the gate, is not
decided.

## The clues (made once, at the next boot)

- **A Crown flask**, hidden in the scree on Red Ridge where the two Keth went down (a hidden find, dc 13 on search,
  survival, lore or track). The ridge's description now mentions something pewter in the scree. The tag on its cord is
  in the notice's hand: "Ninth night. Three down of thirst ... Raised, and their skins filled. The ration is set and
  posted at the cistern for the gate. The Host."
- **Wick the runner** saw a rider go up the road that night with no lamp and a crown over a shut eye on the saddle
  cloth (ask him about the rider, the ninth, the night, a lamp, the Host, the notice or the eye).
- **Dram Hollis** remembers the fine Dreyer paid for the man at the back gate was entered "to the Host".
- **Ossa** remembers the Guild charter holds the Reach's water rights "under the Host of the Crown".
- **Elder Taan** knows the old name and what the Host has done before, and tells only someone who has kept faith.

## Not built

- Anyone seeing the Host. The next time a body goes down of thirst where nobody can help it, a rider could come.
- How the Host ties to the back gate and the torn pages.
