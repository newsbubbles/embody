When the engine breaks, the world remembers.

A fault in the engine is a real event in the world: it is in the log, and the bodies that saw it remember it. The host fixes the engine and makes the wronged whole, but does not erase what was lived. Two histories, both true.

1 · Maintenance · the host

A fault, then a fix

Something slipped past a boundary nobody meant to open. The host closes it in the engine, repairs the harm with the same ops an author builds with, and writes down what happened, outside the world.

3 · Experience · what they saw

Hollow men from the Scar

Each body saw only what reached it: men in familiar coats, no voice, no reason, blows out of the dark. Then the attackers broke to sand, and the dead woke up.

Nobody in the world is told it was a bug. Their memory is true to what they saw.

A tale the world tells itself

Minds compare what they saw, give the thing a name, fear it and go looking. A fault becomes folklore, and the host can build on it: the next quest starts from what they believe.

Keep the history. Repair what was unfair. Say so outside the world.

Play
