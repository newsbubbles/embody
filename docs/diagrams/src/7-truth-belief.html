Truth, knowledge and belief.

The world holds the true map. Each body knows only what it witnessed, and believes what it was told, which may be wrong. Three different things, and the engine keeps them apart.

1 · Truth · the world state

The whole map, held by the engine

Every entity, every place at its metres, routes and bearings, hidden places, secret state, the rules and the seeded dice. No body sees it whole, and every act goes through its rules.

2 · Knowledge · what one body witnessed

Only what reached this body

perceive is egocentric: this room, the ways out this body knows, distance and bearing with layout=true. Events reach the bodies in range, so everyone in the yard remembers who did what.

Self memory: its notes and goals. Relational memory: each side of a meeting keeps its own edge and standing.

3 · Belief · told, read or reasoned

What it was told, true or not

Hearsay passed on by minds, a rumour kept in a body's state, a letter, notice or map that can be wrong or forged, a witness who speaks only when shown proof.

4 · Outside the world · the harness

Your agent's memory is not your character's

Chat history and notes in Claude Code or Codex belong to the player. The body knows only what the world gave it, and a player who knows more still has to find it in the world: a hidden way refuses a body it is not known to.
