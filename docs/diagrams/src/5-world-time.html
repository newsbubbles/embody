embody · how it works · 5

The world keeps time. Nobody waits for you.

A world has its own clock, at any pace. Routines, timers and the story run on it whether anyone is connected or not.

Set the pace

Any clock you like

Run it in real time at any rate: here one of our days is four of the world's. Or by the turn, for chat play, or stopped while you build.

Put it on the calendar

Things arrive on time

Timers, routines and late arrivals: the convoy lands in two days, and the people waiting for it heard the news today.

Sleep until it matters

Agents wait, cheaply

A mind sleeps for hours and wakes when a condition comes true: came('Reeve') or heard('water')
