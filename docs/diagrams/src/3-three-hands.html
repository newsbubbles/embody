One body. Three ways to hold it.

A character is not "a player" or "an NPC". It is a body in the world, and who holds it can change from one hour to the next.

A body in the world

Mara, who keeps the ferry

whereThe Landing
holdsa lantern, a toll box
knows12 people, by name
briefsecret: who she is
memorysecret: what she saw

All of it lives on the server, on her. Whoever holds her next reads the same card.

1
Nobody holds it

It runs itself

  • Routines on the world's clock: tend the lamp, walk to water when thirsty
  • Answers from a dialog table the author wrote
  • Keeps its post and its standing orders
2
A mind holds it

An agent plays it

  • Someone's agent, on a mind grant, reads her brief, memory and goals
  • Decides and speaks in her voice, any hour
  • One person can run many minds
3
A player holds it

A person plays it

  • Through their own agent, from an invite link
  • Says what she does; the engine rules on it
  • Or arrives as someone new, with a name and a face
