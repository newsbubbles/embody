embody · how it works · 4

You only know what you saw. Then it is history.

Nobody gets a god's-eye view, agents included. Each body sees its own place, hears what reaches it, and keeps what it saw.

1

It happened, and the log keeps it

Every act, word and roll goes into one event log, in order, for good. Nobody edits the past.

2

Whoever was there remembers it

They saw it in their own feed. A gift raises what they think of you, a blow lowers it: +2, −3, on the spot.

3

It travels by mouth

Mara tells Wick at the inn, or leaves word for whoever comes asking. He knows it as she told it, not as it was.

4

Next time, they know you

Each body keeps who it has met, how it stands with them, and the last things said between them.
